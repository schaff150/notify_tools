📧 Email-to-SMS (Gmail → Carrier Gateway)

Send texts via Gmail SMTP to your carrier's SMS gateway. No Android app needed — runs entirely server-side. Uses a Gmail App Password (not your regular password).

How it works: Your phone number (e.g. +15551234567) is emailed to 15551234567@msg.fi.google.com and Google Fi delivers it as a text. Change the gateway domain if you're on a different carrier.

The Gmail account that sends the notifications.

Generate at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. Requires 2FA enabled on your Google account.

Shows as the sender name.

Google Fi: msg.fi.google.com · Verizon: vtext.com · AT&T: txt.att.net · T-Mobile: tmomail.net

📱 SMS Gateway for Android (Legacy)

Install the free SMS Gateway app on your Android phone. Open the app, tap Local server, and copy the credentials shown on the Home screen.

Local mode (recommended): Base URL is http://[phone-ip]:8080 — texts send from your own phone number over LAN.

Cloud mode: Use https://api.sms-gate.app — works away from home but routes through sms-gate.app cloud.

Shown in the app under Local server or use https://api.sms-gate.app for cloud mode.

ℹ️ How It Works

Every morning between 06:00–12:00, Famguessr picks a random city from ~500 worldwide locations and sends each family member a text with the local time at that place.

Requirements: Email-to-SMS must be configured in Settings. Each family member needs a phone number in the Notify Tag Map (Jellyfin tab).
Fallback: If the WorldTimeAPI is unreachable, the message will say "local time" instead of a specific time. The daily reminder still goes out.
